# Prompts for the cover art

For an image model that takes reference pictures. Attach the reference
pictures named with each prompt (`brand/reference/`). Ask for one shape
at a time. The title is never the model's: it is set afterwards from
`game/assets/brand/drift-fever-wordmark.svg` by `game/test/brand/compose.js`.

## The car, said once and used in every prompt

Attach `studio-front-three-quarter.png`, `studio-side.png` and
`studio-rear-three-quarter.png`.

> These pictures show an original fictional car from my own video game.
> Keep THIS car: a low, wide, wedge-shaped two-door coupe in deep amber
> yellow with a flat black roof and dark glass, a long flat bonnet, thin
> horizontal slit headlights, a black front splitter and side skirts, a
> very large black rear wing on two tall posts with amber end plates, and
> black wheels with three bright red blades each. Violet neon underglow.
> It must not look like any real production car, and it must carry no
> badges, logos, number plates or lettering of any kind.

## 1. Landscape, 16:9 (the main cover and the share card)

> Using the attached car exactly as described, paint dramatic key art
> for an arcade drift racing game. The car is caught mid-drift at a steep
> angle, front three-quarter view, low camera close to the road, front
> wheels turned hard into the slide, thick white tyre smoke pouring off
> the rear wheels and curling behind it, sparks, violet underglow
> lighting the wet tarmac. Night circuit under floodlights with a glowing
> orange and purple sunset still on the horizon, grandstand lights
> blurred in the background, strong motion blur on the track. Stylised,
> polished 3D illustration with crisp faceted bodywork, rich saturated
> colour, cinematic rim lighting, high detail. The car fills the lower
> two thirds and sits slightly right of centre. Leave the top third as
> clean, darker sky with nothing in it, for a title to be added later.
> No text, no letters, no logos, no watermark, no border, no people, no
> user interface. 16:9.

## 2. Portrait, 2:3

> Same car, same style and same scene as before, recomposed as a tall
> 2:3 poster. The car drifts towards the camera in the lower half, front
> three-quarter view, smoke rising up and behind it, violet underglow on
> the road. The upper 40 per cent is clean dark sky with a soft orange
> and purple glow at the horizon and nothing else in it, for a title to
> be added later. No text, no letters, no logos, no watermark, no
> border. 2:3 portrait.

## 3. Square, 1:1

> Same car, same style and same scene, recomposed as a square. The car
> is large and centred in the lower 60 per cent, mid-drift, front
> three-quarter view, smoke behind. The top 35 per cent is clean dark
> sky for a title to be added later. No text, no letters, no logos, no
> watermark, no border. 1:1.

## 4. A second idea: two cars

Attach `action-front-landscape.png` as well, for the feel of the game.

> Using the attached amber car exactly as described, paint key art of a
> drift battle: the amber car in front, sideways at full angle, and a
> second fictional low-poly coupe in lime green drifting door to door
> just behind it, both pouring smoke, at dusk on a race circuit with a
> purple and orange sky. Same stylised, polished, faceted 3D look, low
> camera, cinematic lighting. Top third clean sky for a title. No text,
> no letters, no logos, no badges, no watermark, no border. 16:9.

## If a result goes wrong

- It looks like a real car: "Remove any resemblance to real cars. Keep
  the flat wedge shape and thin slit headlights from the reference."
- It adds writing or badges: "Remove all text, numbers, logos and
  plates."
- The wing or wheels change: "Match the reference: tall black wing with
  amber end plates, black wheels with three red blades."
- No room for the title: "Move the car down and leave the top third as
  empty sky."
- Too photographic: "More stylised and faceted, like the reference; a
  game illustration, not a photograph."
